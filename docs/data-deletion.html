User Data Deletion

Last updated: March 4, 2026

Your Rights

In accordance with Meta Platform requirements and data protection regulations, you have the right to request complete deletion of your data from our system.

Data We May Store

  • Instagram user identifier
  • Message history with the bot (up to 30 days)
  • Interaction timestamps

How to Request Deletion

Option 1: Send an email to info@jera.ai with the subject "Data Deletion" and include your Instagram account name.

Option 2: Send a message on Telegram: @jera_ai

Processing Time

We will process your request within 5 business days. After deletion, you will receive confirmation at the contact you provided.

What Happens After Deletion

  • All your data will be permanently removed from our system
  • Your message history with the bot will be erased
  • You may continue using the bot — in that case, data collection will start anew
