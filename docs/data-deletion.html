User Data Deletion

Last updated: March 4, 2026

Your Rights

In accordance with Meta Platform requirements, GDPR, CCPA, and other data protection regulations, you have the right to request complete deletion of your data from our systems.

Data We May Store

  • Platform user identifier (e.g., Instagram Scoped ID)
  • Message history with the bot (up to 30 days)
  • Booking and appointment records
  • Interaction timestamps

How to Request Deletion

Option 1: Send an email to hello@jera.ai with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your platform account name or user ID.

Option 2: Call us at +1 (415) 555-0123 during business hours.

Processing Time

We will process your request within 5 business days. After deletion is complete, you will receive confirmation at the contact method you provided.

What Happens After Deletion

  • All your personal data will be permanently removed from our systems
  • Your message history with the bot will be erased
  • Any booking records associated with your account will be anonymized
  • You may continue using the bot — in that case, data collection will start anew

Automatic Deletion

Even without a manual request, we automatically delete conversation data after 30 days of inactivity. Booking records are retained for 12 months for business continuity purposes, then permanently deleted.
