Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Jera AIChatBot ("Service", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects user information when interacting with our chatbot on Instagram.

2. Data We Collect

When using our chatbot, we may receive the following information:

  • Instagram user identifier (Instagram Scoped ID)
  • Text of messages sent to the bot
  • Message timestamps

We do not collect or store: passwords, payment information, precise location, contacts, or photographs.

3. How We Use Your Data

The collected data is used exclusively for:

  • Processing incoming messages and generating responses
  • Improving the quality of the chatbot

Data is processed automatically and is not shared with third parties.

4. Data Retention

Messages are processed in real time and are not permanently stored on our servers. Logs may be retained for up to 30 days for debugging purposes, after which they are automatically deleted.

5. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data, including the use of encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to data.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request information about your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Stop using the service at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at: info@jera.ai

7. Data Deletion

To request data deletion, visit our data deletion page or email us at info@jera.ai.

8. Use of Meta Platform

Our service uses the Instagram Messaging API by Meta Platforms, Inc. Data usage complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Meta Developer Policy.
