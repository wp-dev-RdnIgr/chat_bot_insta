Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Jera ("Service", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects user information when interacting with our AI-powered chatbots and automation services, including bots deployed on Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and Telegram.

2. Data We Collect

When using our chatbot, we may receive the following information:

  • Platform user identifier (e.g., Instagram Scoped ID)
  • Text of messages sent to the bot
  • Message timestamps
  • Basic profile information provided by the platform (name, profile picture URL)

We do not collect or store: passwords, payment card details, precise geolocation, contacts, or photographs.

3. How We Use Your Data

The collected data is used exclusively for:

  • Processing incoming messages and generating responses
  • Managing bookings and appointments
  • Improving the quality and accuracy of the chatbot

Data is processed automatically and is not shared with third parties except as required to deliver the service (e.g., calendar integrations, CRM systems authorized by our clients).

4. Data Retention

Messages are processed in real time. Conversation logs may be retained for up to 30 days for debugging and service improvement purposes, after which they are automatically deleted.

5. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data, including the use of encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), restricted access controls, and secure cloud infrastructure.

  • Stop using the service at any time
